======================================================================
HOW TO DEPLOY
======================================================================
HOSTINGER / cPANEL
  1. Open File Manager, go into public_html.
  2. Turn on "Show hidden files" so .htaccess is not missed.
  3. Delete or back up what is there now (WordPress site = take a
     full backup first).
  4. Upload EVERYTHING that is INSIDE the public_html folder in this
     zip: the 8 .html files, the assets folder, .htaccess,
     robots.txt and sitemap.xml.
     index.html must sit directly inside public_html, not inside a
     sub folder.
  5. Visit https://thedelhipalace.ca and click through the menu.

CLOUDFLARE (DinePalace/Delhipalace repo)
  Copy everything inside public_html into the repo's public/ folder,
  push to main. The repo's own wrangler.jsonc already handles clean
  URLs and the 404 page. .htaccess is ignored there, which is fine.

PREVIEW ON YOUR OWN COMPUTER
  Double click public_html/index.html. All menu links work.
