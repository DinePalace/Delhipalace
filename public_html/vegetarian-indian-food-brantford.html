Vegetarian Indian food in Brantford

Paneer, tandoori veg, momos and more — how to eat vegetarian (very well) at Delhi Palace.

Home / Blog / Vegetarian Guide

Indian cuisine has one of the deepest vegetarian traditions in the world, and our menu reflects it: whole sections of vegetarian appetizers, mains, tandoori and Hakka dishes — not an afterthought page. Here’s how to eat meat-free at Delhi Palace in Brantford.

Start with the appetizers

Vegetable pakora — golden chickpea-battered fritters, currently in our 25% off featured items. Veg spring rolls, papri chaat — crisp wafers with yogurt, chutneys and chickpeas — and veg momos, hand-folded and steamed, with chilli dipping sauce.

Paneer, the main event

Paneer — fresh Indian cottage cheese — anchors the vegetarian mains. Kadhai paneer comes wok-tossed with peppers and crushed coriander; paneer tikka masala brings the creamy tomato treatment; and on the Hakka side, chilli paneer delivers the chilli-garlic hit that made chilli chicken famous, meat-free.

Beyond paneer

Dal (slow-cooked lentils), chana masala, mixed vegetable curries and aloo dishes fill out the Indian veg mains, with vegetable biryani for a one-pot dinner. From the tandoor: veg skewers and every bread — naan, garlic naan, roti — baked fresh to order.

Vegetarian Hakka

The whole Hakka playbook works vegetarian: veg Hakka noodles, veg fried rice, veg Manchurian and chilli paneer. If Indo-Chinese food is new to you, our Hakka guide explains the cuisine.

Finish sweet

Gulab jamun — warm milk dumplings in rose-scented syrup — is vegetarian, beloved, and also on the 25% off list. Pair with a mango lassi.
