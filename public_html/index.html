Frequently asked questions

What are Delhi Palace's opening hours?
We are open every day of the week, Monday to Sunday, from 11:15 am to 10:00 pm — for dine-in, pickup and delivery.
Does Delhi Palace offer delivery and pickup in Brantford?
Yes. Order directly from us online for delivery or pickup — no third-party markups — or call us at +1 (519) 304 9661. You can also order through our app on the App Store and Google Play.
What kind of food does Delhi Palace serve?
Authentic Indian and Hakka (Indo-Chinese) cuisine: tandoori specialties, curries like butter chicken and kadhai paneer, biryanis, momos and dumplings, Hakka noodles and chilli dishes, fresh naan and breads, plus Indian sweets and drinks. See the full menu.
Are there vegetarian options?
Plenty. The menu has dedicated vegetarian sections — Indian vegetarian mains, tandoori veg, vegetable appetizers, paneer dishes and vegetarian Hakka favourites like chilli paneer and veg Hakka noodles.
Where is Delhi Palace located?
At 46 Dalhousie St in downtown Brantford, Ontario (N3T 2H8) — a short walk from Harmony Square. Dine in with us, or order pickup and delivery.

What is Hakka cuisine?
Hakka is Indo-Chinese cooking — Chinese wok technique meeting Indian spice. Think chilli chicken, chilli paneer, Hakka noodles, fried rice and Manchurian dishes: fiery, garlicky and wok-tossed to order. Read more in our guide to Hakka cuisine.
