About Us

Where great food brings people together

Welcome to Delhi Palace, Authentic Indian and Hakka Cuisine, Brantford's destination for exceptional Indian and Hakka flavours. Located in the heart of the city, we're proud to bring the rich culinary traditions of India and the bold, vibrant tastes of Hakka cuisine to our community and visitors alike.

Delhi Palace is more than just a restaurant. It's a place where friends and families come together to share great food and create lasting memories. We take pride in a welcoming atmosphere where guests can relax, celebrate special occasions, or simply enjoy a delicious meal with loved ones.

Authentic Traditions

Unforgettable flavours, every plate

Our chefs use fresh ingredients, authentic spices, and traditional cooking techniques so every meal reflects the true essence of Indian and Hakka cuisine. Whether you prefer mild and comforting flavours or bold and spicy creations, our diverse menu has something for every palate.

We invite you to discover the rich flavours of India and Hakka cuisine and enjoy a true culinary journey, right here in Brantford.

A Culinary Journey

A journey through India's rich culinary heritage

At Delhi Palace, food is more than a meal. It's a celebration of India's rich culinary heritage. Our chefs combine traditional recipes, aromatic spices, and the finest ingredients to create authentic dishes that capture the true flavours of India. From sizzling tandoori specialties and flavourful curries to freshly baked naan and fragrant biryanis, every dish is prepared with care and passion.
