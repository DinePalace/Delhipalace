Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect the information you share with us.

Home / Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you place an order, book a table, or contact us, we collect only what we need to serve you: your name, phone number, email address, and where relevant your delivery address and order details.

How we use it

We use your information to prepare and deliver your order, to contact you about that order, and to answer your questions. If you opt in, we may also send you occasional offers from Delhi Palace. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the service providers who help us operate, such as our online ordering and payment providers, and only to the extent they need it to do that work.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to delhipalacebrantford@gmail.com or call +1 (519) 304 9661 and we will take care of it.
